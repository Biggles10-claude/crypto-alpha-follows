Build lists, not noise

Make separate X Lists for deal-flow, on-chain, security, research, trading-ms and APAC. Mute “100x”, “DM me”, “VIP” and “guaranteed”. Pin EmberCN, ai_9684xtpa, WuBlockchain, mrjasonchoi and StalkHQ for the Perth morning tape.
