## Fund employees as a firehose (method ≠ list)

`@ardizor`-style “follow every Tier-1 associate” dumps (Paradigm/a16z/Multicoin junior lists) are a **discovery method**, not a curated 50. Including dozens of associates would (a) duplicate VC education follows, (b) create follow-noise, (c) go stale when staff churn. We kept a few high-signal GPs/researchers (`FrankieIsLost`, `_charlienoyes`, `Rewkang`, `ZeMariaMacedo`) instead.
